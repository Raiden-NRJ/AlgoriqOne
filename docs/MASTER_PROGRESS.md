| 2026-08-13 | **Whitespace pass — the two section-rhythm clamps retuned, and every off-scale gap in the section components brought onto the scale.** A cross-cutting spacing refinement over built work (M0+M1), not a numbered doc. **Measured first, in a browser, against the production build** — the source alone cannot show this, because the offender is paid *twice* at every boundary (bottom padding of section N + top padding of section N+1) and so is invisible in any single file. Ranked at 375/768/1280/1920 across the homepage and four sub-pages: **(a) section padding was 24.3% of the homepage's entire scroll height at 1920px** and 20.2% at 1280 — boundary gaps of 256px at 1280 and **336px at 1920** — against (b) heading-to-content gaps of 56–64px, (c) card-grid gaps of 24–40px, and (d) Container padding, which is not an offender at all (`max-w` governs at ≥1280, so the 48px gutter never binds there).
