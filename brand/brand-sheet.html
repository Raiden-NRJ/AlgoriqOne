The mark

An angular A knocked out of a rounded container. The container reads as One — a single system holding the whole chain — and doubles as the app icon, avatar and favicon. The A is a knockout rather than a separate shape, so the mark is a single path and works on any background in a single colour.

Primary
One colour
Reversed
Cyan · dark only

Lockup & clear space

Clear space on all sides equals the height of the container's corner radius — a quarter of the mark's height. Never place type, rules or image edges inside it. In prose the product is always Algoryq One, two words; AlgoryqOne is the code identifier only.

Horizontal lockup · minimum clear space

Minimum sizes

Tested, not assumed. The mark holds its counter down to 16 px, which is the favicon floor. Below 16 px use the container alone.

Colour

Three brand colours. Contrast ratios are computed from the tokens, not estimated. Cyan is a supporting colour only — it never carries text on a light surface.

Usage

DoUse the single-colour version by default. The mark is designed to work in one colour.
Don'tAdd gradients, shadows, bevels or glows to the mark.
DoKeep the A as a true knockout so the background shows through.
Don'tFill the A with a second colour — it becomes two shapes and breaks at small sizes.
DoUse cyan for the mark only on the dark band.
Don'tSet the mark in cyan on white — 2.3:1 fails both text and non-text contrast.
DoKeep the container's proportions locked; scale uniformly.
Don'tStretch, rotate, re-space the lockup, or rebuild the A by hand.
